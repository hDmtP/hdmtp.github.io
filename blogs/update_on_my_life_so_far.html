Update on my life so far..

19-10-2026 Bhubaneshwar

It's been more than 3 years...

Hello to any one reading this
  • It's almost over 3.5 years I haven't even touched this blog. Lot's of things have happened within this time. I could make like 20 seperate blogs regarding that. But I will keep it brief. And then highlight few of the striking things that have impacted my life so far.
  • I finally graduate somewhere in July 2024 from my college. The period of November-December 2024 was probably some of the most impactful and greatest time of my life so far. I won't go into detail, but it was like a serene discovery, achieving the highest form of clarity imaginable. Some ultimate realizations that I couldn't even write down here for now.
  • After struggling with the withdrawl symptoms throughout the month of Nov 2024 from a lifetime of caffeine addiction, I finally quitted caffeine and I am still caffeine free as of now.
  • From Dec 2024 to Dec 2025 I did internship at National Stock Exchange. I am trying to cram a whole year under an internship here but again, there have been other significant discoveries made by me, about life in general as a continuation of the clarity I had gained end of 2024.
  • And as of currently, I am working in IBM as an Application Developer. Joined in May 18, 2026. Around 2 months in BCIT Bangalore for induction and JRS training and the final assessment. And then from Bangalore to Bhubaneswar and for the last 3 months or so I am here. Been to my home 3 times already! I might get into the details and rants in later blogs but not here.
